Which object is it, after it disappears?

Video-language models lose track of an object once the evidence that identified it is gone. Track, Then Bind asks the model about identity once, at the moment the evidence is visible, lets a tracker carry that identity, and answers from the tracked state. Every example below can be inspected: the moment of the identity decision, the numbered tracks the model saw, and the facts behind the answer.
