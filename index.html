I am a Ph.D. student in the Department of Data Science at The College of William & Mary, supervised by Dr. Yanhai Xiong. Previously, I obtained my M.Sc. in Electrical Engineering from the National University of Singapore (NUS) (advised by Dr. John S. Ho) and B.Eng. in Microelectronic Science and Engineering from University of Electronic Science and Technology of China (UESTC).

Prior to my Ph.D., I worked as a Digital Logic Engineer at Cambricon Technologies, focusing on the verification and latency optimization of large-scale AI accelerator chips. Currently, I am also a research collaborator at SparcAI (advised by Dr. Yufei Wang).

My research interests encompass Deep Reinforcement Learning (PPO, SAC, GRPO), Vision-Language-Action (VLA) Models, Multi-Agent Systems & 3D Simulation Environments, and LLM Inference Optimization (Speculative Decoding & KV Cache).

Research Focus

Core research pillars spanning reinforcement learning, multimodal decision-making, and simulation

⚡

Deep RL & LLM Inference

Formulating speculative decoding draft tree expansion as an MDP, applying PPO, SAC & GRPO with Behavior Cloning, and solving KV Cache slippage to maximize Tokens-Per-Second (TPS).

🎯

VLA & Decision Making

Developing Vision-Language-Action frameworks for large-scale Combinatorial Optimization (MIS, VRP, Bin Packing) to overcome spatial grounding limits of pure VLMs.

🌊

3D Simulation & MAPF

Building realistic simulation platforms (UP-Bench for underwater dynamics) and novel Multi-Agent Pathfinding architectures (LENS) decoupling macro U-Net spatial reasoning from collision avoidance.

🚀

Generative AI & Distillation

Accelerating 3D diffusion pipelines (Trellis2 3D generation) via non-data-driven FreeFlow distillation methods, achieving over 2.34x speedup without paired datasets.

UP-Bench: A Benchmark for Underwater Path Planning Algorithms

🏆 KDD 2025 Oral
Di Yang, Yanhai Xiong
ACM SIGKDD Conference on Knowledge Discovery and Data Mining (KDD 2025, Benchmark Track Oral)
A highly configurable open-source 3D simulation benchmark platform incorporating realistic fluid dynamics, partial observability, and dynamic obstacles. Comprehensive benchmarking of learning-based Deep Reinforcement Learning (SAC) and classical planning algorithms.
