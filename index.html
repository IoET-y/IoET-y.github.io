Research Interests

World Models & 4D Representation

Generative spatiotemporal world models and next-generation 4D state representations capturing physics and dynamic environment semantics.

World Action Models

Action-grounded predictive world models for continuous control, multi-step planning, and physical interaction.

Multi-Agent Systems & Simulation

Decentralized multi-agent coordination (LENS, TMLR) and realistic 3D simulation platforms (UP-Bench, KDD Oral).

Few-Step 3D Distillation

High-resolution native 3D generation (Channel Match for TRELLIS.2) with custom JVP FlashAttention kernels and latent calibration.

Selected Publications & Manuscripts

Channel Match: Calibrating Shape Latents for Few-Step 3D Generation
Di Yang et al.
Research Manuscript, 2026 Preprint
A few-step distillation framework for native 3D flow models enabled by continuous self-consistency on sparse structured latents with a custom JVP FlashAttention kernel. Channel Match calibrates sampling-induced latent distribution drift, achieving 4.5× faster geometry sampling on TRELLIS.2 (1024³ resolution) while restoring downstream PBR texture quality without retraining.

HyCO: A Hybrid Neural Solver for Combinatorial Optimization
Di Yang et al.
Research Manuscript, 2026 Under Review

Di Yang, Yanhai Xiong, et al.
