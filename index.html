Prior to my Ph.D., I served as a Digital Logic Engineer at Cambricon Technologies, specializing in the verification and latency optimization of large-scale AI accelerator chips.

My current research focuses on World Models, World Action Models, Multi-Agent World Models, and Next-Generation 4D World State Representations, alongside foundational 3D Generation & Simulation and Deep Reinforcement Learning.

Research Interests

Pioneering world modeling, action representation, and 4D state dynamics

🌐

World Models & 4D Representation

Building predictive generative world models and next-generation 4D world state representations that capture spatiotemporal physics and environment dynamics for autonomous systems.

🎯

World Action Models

Formulating action-conditioned world models that ground continuous actions and high-dimensional decisions into predictive latent trajectories for multi-step reasoning and control.

👥

Multi-Agent World Models

Investigating multi-agent coordination and decentralized decision-making in complex partially observable worlds (LENS, TMLR) and realistic 3D simulation platforms (UP-Bench, KDD Oral).

⚡

Few-Step 3D Distillation

Accelerating native 3D foundation models (Channel Match for TRELLIS.2) via continuous self-consistency, custom fused JVP FlashAttention kernels, and training-free latent statistics alignment.

Selected Publications & Manuscripts

Peer-reviewed papers, accepted articles, and preprints

Channel Match: Calibrating Shape Latents for Few-Step 3D Generation

📄 Manuscript (2026)
Di Yang et al.
Research Manuscript, 2026
Presents a scalable few-step distillation framework for native 3D flow models enabled by continuous self-consistency training on sparse structured 3D latents with a custom JVP FlashAttention kernel. Discovers and solves sampling-induced latent distribution drift via Channel Match, achieving 4.5× faster geometry sampling on TRELLIS.2 (1024³ resolution) while restoring downstream PBR texture quality without retraining.
Preprint / PDF

LENS: Learning to Navigate with Active Search for Partially Observable MAPF in Unknown Environments

🎉 Accepted by TMLR (2026)
Di Yang, Yanhai Xiong, et al.
Transactions on Machine Learning Research (TMLR), 2026 (Accepted)
Proposes a hybrid Multi-Agent Pathfinding (MAPF) architecture decoupling macro-topological spatial reasoning (via U-Nets) from micro-level collision avoidance, achieving out-of-distribution generalization comparable to 85M-parameter foundation models with <0.2% training data.

UP-Bench: A Benchmark for Underwater Path Planning Algorithms

🏆 KDD 2025 Oral
Di Yang, Yanhai Xiong
ACM SIGKDD Conference on Knowledge Discovery and Data Mining (KDD 2025, Benchmark Track Oral)
A highly configurable open-source 3D simulation benchmark platform incorporating realistic fluid dynamics, partial observability, and dynamic obstacles. Evaluates diverse Deep Reinforcement Learning (SAC) and classical planning algorithms in complex 3D environments.
